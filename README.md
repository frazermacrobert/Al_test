# Blue Manor

A mobile-friendly roguelike mansion game inspired by Blue Prince.

Each night the manor rearranges. Each day you draft its rooms from random blueprints, manage your limited steps, and search for the hidden 46th room.

## How to Play

- **Draft rooms** — at each unopened door, choose one of three blueprints to place
- **Manage steps** — every move costs a step; run out and the day ends
- **Find synergies** — Nursery + Bedrooms, Ballroom + wide rooms, Solarium + blue rooms
- **Avoid red rooms** — or use the Shelter and Utility Closet to counter them
- **Chase blue streaks** — draft 8 consecutive blue rooms to break the seal
- **Discover secrets** — search walls, drain fountains, look through telescopes
- **Find Room 46** — unlock the Vault and the Blue Seal to reveal the hidden chamber

## Features

- 40+ unique rooms with distinct effects and interactions
- Room drafting with Ivory Dice rerolls
- Daily seed — different blueprints each real-world day
- Progressive unlocks — new rooms appear as you play more runs
- 9 secrets and 8 synergies to discover across runs
- Persistent journal tracking your discoveries
- Touch-friendly mobile-first design
- Single HTML file, no dependencies

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build steps needed.
